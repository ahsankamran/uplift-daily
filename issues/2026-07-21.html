Small Wonders · Gulf of Guinea, Benin

A coral reef presumed dead for sixty years turned out to be teeming with life off Benin.

Scientists surveying the seabed off Benin's coast in the Gulf of Guinea found a living mesophotic coral reef that 1960s surveys had written off as dead, identifying eight coral species and eight fish species across seven miles of seabed. Gérard Zinzindohoué and colleagues at Benin's Institute of Halieutic and Oceanographic Research in Cotonou used sonar and dive surveys to confirm the find, and suspect the reef may once have stretched 40 kilometers along the shelf. The team is now mapping its full extent to help Benin decide how to protect a habitat nobody knew was still there.

Nature · Xishuangbanna, Yunnan, China

A wandering elephant family that captivated China in 2021 has grown to twenty-one members.

The "short-nose family," an Asian elephant herd in Yunnan's Xishuangbanna Dai Autonomous Prefecture, has grown from 14 to 21 members since 2021, with two calves born this year alone. The herd became known worldwide after wandering more than 1,400 kilometers across Yunnan that year before returning home; rangers now track it with an early-warning system that has issued over 210,000 alerts since 2022 to keep elephants and farmers apart. China's wild Asian elephant population has climbed from about 150 in the 1980s to nearly 400 today, aided by 11 nature reserves and dedicated feeding grounds known as "elephant canteens."

Global Progress · Sub-Saharan Africa

A malaria vaccine that costs about £2.50 a dose is now reaching more than twenty African countries.

The R21/Matrix-M vaccine, which showed 75 to 80 percent protection in trials, has moved into routine immunization programs across more than 20 African countries since Ghana first approved it in 2023. Oxford researcher Adrian Hill, who began the work after witnessing malaria's toll on children in the Gambia in 1988, was awarded the European Inventor Award 2026 for developing it with the Serum Institute of India and Novavax; each dose costs about £2.50 and keeps for two years without special refrigeration. That combination of low cost and easy storage is what is letting health systems in remote areas start using it at scale.

Quiet Kindness · Concord, North Carolina

A lonely four-year-old's daily wave turned his Concord, North Carolina block into a real neighborhood.

After his parents' divorce left him without playmates, four-year-old Roman started waving at every neighbor he saw from his driveway in Concord, North Carolina. Retiree Wade Folger and others began waving back, then stopping to talk, until people who had lived next to each other for years finally learned one another's names; CBS correspondent Steve Hartman documented the change for On the Road. Roman's mother, Anna Butzloff, says the block now checks in on each other the way neighbors used to.

Everyday Joy · Venice, Italy

Venice filled its lagoon with 2,500 decorated boats for a tradition that turns 450 this year.

On the night of July 18, more than 2,500 gondolas and motorboats strung with lanterns, balloons and flowers gathered in St. Mark's Basin for the Festa del Redentore, Venice's summer festival marking its 450th year. Families anchored together for a floating picnic before a 45-minute fireworks display lit the basin at 11:30 p.m., followed the next day by traditional two-oar gondola and pupparino races. A temporary votive bridge across the Giudecca Canal stayed open through the weekend so residents could walk to the Church of the Redeemer on foot, just as they have every July for four and a half centuries.

Science & Medicine · Brazil

A cheap antidepressant eased long COVID fatigue in a Brazilian trial of 399 adults.

In a randomized trial across 22 outpatient clinics in Brazil, adults who had been fatigued for at least 90 days after a COVID-19 infection felt significantly less tired after 60 days on fluvoxamine than those given a placebo, with the improvement still holding at day 90. The REVIVE-TOGETHER trial, run by Brazilian researchers and published this month, tested the decades-old antidepressant against metformin and a placebo, finding fluvoxamine alone produced a statistically significant benefit. Because fluvoxamine is off-patent and inexpensive, doctors could start prescribing it for long COVID fatigue immediately rather than waiting years for a new drug.

Quiet Kindness · London, England

A daughter's TikTok video saved her father's 30-year-old shoe shop.

A TikTok video about a rack of Y2K kitten heels drew 300,000 views overnight and sent two-hour queues to a small vintage shoe shop in Shoreditch, London.

Nature · Tongjiang, China

China and Russia released nearly half a million sturgeon into their shared border river.

Biologists in Tongjiang, on China's border with Russia, released 200 Amur sturgeon and 485,000 kaluga into the Amur River on July 14, along with 5.2 million fry of other native fish.

Global Progress · Burkina Faso

Burkina Faso just cut malaria deaths among young children by 60 percent in a year.

Malaria cases in Burkina Faso fell 32 percent between 2024 and 2025, to about 7.3 million, while deaths dropped 44 percent to just under 2,000.
