Human Spirit · Delhi, India · November 4, 2026
Photo: Samiul Haque Bhuyan, via Unsplash

A Delhi theatre fellowship pays its trainees 15,000 rupees a month to learn the craft.

Bargad, a free six-month theatre fellowship in Delhi, will pay each of the 14 artists in its January 2027 cohort 15,000 rupees a month, up from the 10,000 rupees its first two cohorts received. Amba Suhasini K Jhala and Anirudh Nair started it in 2025 with two other Delhi theatre-makers, running classes in Hindi from 4pm to 8.30pm six days a week and funding the whole thing from roughly 20 to 25 individual donors; the stipend exists so that applicants from outside theatre families can tell their relatives the work pays. Applications grew from about 75 in the first year to around 200 in the second, and one graduate has since entered the National School of Drama.

Sydney, Australia · November 3, 2026

A stranger carried an Irish player's spare wheelchair to Australia in her own luggage.

Deirdre Donoghue flew a custom-built rugby league wheelchair from Ireland to Sydney in her checked baggage and handed it over at the international terminal on 3 November. She had phoned RTE after hearing an on-air appeal from the uncle of 20-year-old Oran Spain, whose only chair cracked during training in Wollongong and whose spare was sitting at home in Ireland; already booked to visit a friend she had not seen in ten years, she kept the chair with her across every connecting flight. Spain, who had sat out Ireland's opening match of the Wheelchair Rugby League World Cup, took the court against Wales that same day, and Donoghue plans to travel down to Wollongong to watch the team.

Kallachi, Kerala, India · November 2, 2026

A Kozhikode auto driver handed in the 1.05 million rupees he found lying on a state highway.

Thayyil Suneesh, an auto-rickshaw driver from Kummamkode, spotted a packet holding 1.05 million rupees, about $12,000, on the state highway in front of the Kairali Complex at Kallachi, in Kerala's Kozhikode district, at around 8pm on 26 October. He took the packet to the Nadapuram police station that same night, and an alert circulated on social media reached the owners, who had lost 22 bundles of 500-rupee notes along the road. They arrived at the station at about 2pm the next day, and Sub-Inspector Sreejith handed the money back to them there.

Yernagudem, Andhra Pradesh, India · November 1, 2026

An engineer working in Abu Dhabi has kept free after-school classes running in 79 Indian villages for 15 years.

An after-school programme across 79 villages in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana has logged 569,419 hours of free tuition for 3,165 children since it opened in August 2011 with 50 pupils in Classes 4 and 5. Bethala Veera Swami, a contracts engineer who works in Abu Dhabi, set it up with childhood friends from his village of Yernagudem; the classes run from 3.30pm to 5.30pm six days a week, and teachers log attendance and call parents when a child stops turning up. PARD India now wants the classes running in 150 villages by 2030.

North Fork John Day Wilderness, Oregon, USA · October 4, 2026

Volunteers hand-sawed 300 fallen trees off Oregon wilderness trails this year.

In Oregon's North Fork John Day Wilderness, volunteers cleared more than 300 downed trees from roughly 40 miles of trail this season, reopening routes that had been blocked for hikers and horseback riders. Retired scientists and other Pendleton-area locals, led by 73-year-old Dan Long, did the work with hand saws under a cooperative agreement between the Umatilla National Forest's North Fork Ranger District and the nonprofit Wallowa Mountains Hells Canyon Trails Association, including cutting through a three-foot-wide Douglas-fir that had fallen across the Granite Creek Trail. Long has returned to do the work every season since retiring in 2020, because the Forest Service no longer has the staff to clear the backlog on its own.
