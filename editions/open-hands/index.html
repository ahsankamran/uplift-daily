A Michigan family gave up 20 acres to make a 240-acre forest permanent.

In Emmet County near Harbor Springs, Michigan, the Little Traverse Conservancy closed on 180 acres of hardwood forest after a $3 million community fundraising campaign, and the Ford family donated an additional 20 acres of its own land outright, permanently protecting 240 contiguous acres from development. The Fords have owned the property since the early 1980s and had let neighbors use it for walking and cross-country skiing for decades; conservancy development officer Emily Hughes said the deal takes any future sale to a developer off the table. The conservancy, which named the tract The Hundred Acre Wood, plans to build a parking area and expand the trail network next year, linking the preserve to an adjoining 80 acres already owned by West Traverse Township.
