Global Progress · Dakar, Senegal · October 4, 2026

Senegal starts manufacturing the world's first rapid test for measles.

Gavi approved deployment of the first-ever rapid diagnostic test for measles on September 30, 2026, with production based in Dakar, Senegal. DIATROPIX, a manufacturing subsidiary of the Institut Pasteur de Dakar, makes the test, which cleared a rigorous evaluation by WHO and was validated by the Public Health Agency of Canada's National Microbiology Laboratory. Up to 500,000 tests a year are expected to roll out starting in Senegal, giving frontline health workers a faster way to confirm outbreaks and direct vaccination campaigns where they're needed most.
