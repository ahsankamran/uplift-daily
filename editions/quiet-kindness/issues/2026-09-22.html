Quiet Kindness · Buckinghamshire, England · September 22, 2026

A retired couple have fostered 59 dogs from Battersea over fifteen years.

Nicole and Gary Wordsworth, both in their 60s, took in their first foster dog, a Staffie named Marcel, in late 2011, and have since opened their Buckinghamshire home to 59 more from Battersea Dogs Home. Stays run from a couple of nights to eighteen months, and the couple pass on what they learn — a dog's favorite toy, how far it likes to walk, when it likes its dinner — so Battersea's staff can match each one to a permanent home. "We love to help get them out of the kennels when they are too stressed and need a quiet spot to perch," the couple said, and they have no plans to stop.
