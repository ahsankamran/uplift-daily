Yueqing, Zhejiang, China · November 18, 2026

Five siblings cut 430 steps into a mountain so their mother could go home.

The old track up to the family house in Yannan village had crumbled and snakes kept appearing along it, so the children hauled stone and mixed cement on the slope themselves between August and October and finished the job before winter. What they built is a 430-step stair that turns eleven times on its way up, with stretches fixed to a sheer rock face, for a mother in her eighties who has lived on that mountain all her life and never took to the city. She goes back each year at the Tomb Sweeping Festival to tend her husband's grave; readers online have taken to calling the stair a filial piety sky ladder.

Bratton, Wiltshire, England · November 17, 2026

A Wiltshire village is putting its green spaces back together by hand.

At Gales Ground in Bratton a contractor has cleared the weeds and the trees and shrubs have arrived, stacked and waiting for volunteers to plant, alongside wildflower and wildlife areas and four new allotment plots cut from the existing allotments. The work follows a questionnaire sent to every household in May about how money from new housing should be spent — younger residents were canvassed too, at the bus stop and in Year 6 at Bratton Primary School — and it has already produced an outdoor table tennis table and a pétanque court on the Village Green and a wheelchair-friendly gate at the recreation ground. An appeal from Cllr Cathy Elliott, who chairs the Village Green volunteers, brought in new helpers including Rob Mears, 19 years in the village; the parish council expects the job to take several years, most of it done by residents.
