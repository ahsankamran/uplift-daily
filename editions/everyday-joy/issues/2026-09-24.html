Everyday Joy · George Town, Penang, Malaysia · September 24, 2026

A youth committee brought lantern-making and dough-figurine carving back to Penang's Mid-Autumn Festival.

On the Esplanade in George Town, organizers revived a full slate of Mid-Autumn customs this week, from lantern-riddle games to mooncake-making demonstrations. The Clan-Associations Youth Committee's Goh Kok Liang added dough-figurine carving, paper-cutting, and Sichuan opera face-changing to old standbys like hopscotch and the wind-fire wheel, drawing families ahead of the festival's September 25 date. The point, he said, is giving young people in a modern port city an easy, everyday way to hold onto a shared tradition.
