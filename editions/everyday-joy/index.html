Everyday Joy · Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, USA · November 18, 2026

Five hundred and seventy people dressed as Rocky Balboa and ran the museum steps.

They assembled at the foot of the Philadelphia Museum of Art on Friday 13 November in the required kit — grey hooded sweatshirt, grey sweatpants, black beanie, black Chuck Taylors — and took a Guinness World Records title for the largest gathering of people dressed as the character, more than doubling the 250 the record asked for. Once the adjudicator had counted everyone, the whole field ran up the steps together for a group photograph. The attempt belongs to Rocky Season, the city's run of events marking fifty years since the first film, and drew people from as far as Brazil and Madrid.

Mangaluru, Karnataka, India · November 17, 2026

A relay of Konkani singers kept going for twelve and a half days.

Starting at 6 am on 3 November at the Town Hall in Mangaluru, 6,789 singers in about 300 troupes worked through more than 4,500 Konkani songs by over 250 composers, none of them repeated, and stopped on 15 November at 12 days, 12 hours and 12 minutes. Guinness World Records confirmed it as the longest singing marathon, past the 11 days, 11 hours and 11 minutes set in Vadtal, Gujarat, in July 2024. Mandd Sobhann, the cultural organisation that ran it, turns 40 this year and dedicated the attempt to its late founder Eric Ozario; the relay was the centrepiece of the first World Konkani Music Mega Convention.

South London, England · November 15, 2026

London's Santa School has been rehearsing Father Christmas for 28 years.

Dozens of Father Christmas performers spent Thursday in a south London hall learning this year's toy trends, how to say Merry Christmas in sign language, and what to do when a child asks for something nobody can promise — a parent home again, a relative well again — where listening matters more than an answer. The Ministry of Fun has run the school for 28 years and places its graduates in shopping centres, department stores and grottoes across Britain and beyond, where the average visit to Santa lasts three minutes and 45 seconds. Its yearly State of Santa survey has bookings for Mrs Claus up 32%, 60% of children still posting paper letters, and mince pies left out on 77% of Christmas Eve plates.

Jászberény, Hungary · November 14, 2026

The Rubik's Cube is being made in Hungary again.

A factory in Jászberény opened this week with an automated line of robotic arms that assemble and test the puzzle, and is expected to turn out up to a million cubes a year. Ernő Rubik, now 82, unveiled it; he built the first cube in 1974 from 27 wooden blocks while teaching design in Budapest, and it went on sale at home in 1977 as the Magic Cube. Around 200 million sold in the boom between 1980 and 1983, and the puzzle is now made again in the country it came from.

Gijón, Asturias, Spain · November 13, 2026

A physics student rapped 200 freestyle battles in a single day.

Gazir, a 24-year-old MC from Gijón who studies physics at the University of Oviedo, took on 250 challengers in 200 consecutive freestyle battles on November 7, winning 197 of them in 11 hours, 23 minutes and 14 seconds. He had 20 seconds between battles and a four-minute break after every 40, won his first 115 in a row before losing the 116th to a rapper called Babi, and burned about 3,000 calories by the reckoning of his fitness ring. Guinness World Records has confirmed the attempt, and the next thing Gazir says he wants to try is freestyling in English.

Hervey Bay, Queensland, Australia · November 12, 2026

A house in Hervey Bay holds 5,373 turtles.

Tania Murphy's home in Hervey Bay, Queensland, contains 5,373 turtle and tortoise things — wooden statues, ceramic figurines, plates, glasses, soft toys — which Guinness World Records has confirmed as the largest collection of its kind anywhere. It began when she was five and would not give back her sister's stuffed turtle, and turned deliberate about 20 years ago; two independent witnesses spent some 17 hours counting on video, and she submitted close to 6,000 files and waited three months for the verdict. She has arranged the house like a museum and will show visitors round by appointment, with 10,000 as the next target: There's always room for more turtles.
